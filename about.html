About the Founder

Garrett Riley.

After graduating the Roberto-Venn School of luthiery, Garrett moved back to his home state of Washington and started his path in guitar building and repair. after first working for Rainsong Graphite Guitars in Woodinville, Wa, Garrett set his sights on more repair and custom building experience. He joined the crew at Mike Lull Custom Guitars/Guitarworks in 2014, honing his skills there for seven years. He then moved on to manage the repair shop at Mike and Mike’s Guitar Bar in 2021, before opening his own repair shop and producing the Riley guitar line in 2022.

Garrett also enjoys motorcycling, winter sports and spending time with his wife Mackensie, and his two daughters
