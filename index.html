The maker

Garrett Riley, founder.

After graduating the Roberto-Venn School of luthiery, Garrett moved back to his home state of Washington and started his path in guitar building and repair. He joined the crew at Mike Lull Custom Guitars/Guitarworks in 2014, honing his skills there for seven years, before opening his own repair shop and producing the Riley guitar line in 2022.

We believe in making a simple instrument exceedingly well.
